# Spooky's Jumpscare Mansion: HD Renovation Manual Archipelago
<img width="460" height="215" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4fc6d63e-6380-4273-95cd-200bf6c50af4" />

A manual apworld for the hit indie game [Spooky's Jumpscare Mansion (HD)](https://store.steampowered.com/app/577690/Spookys_Jump_Scare_Mansion_HD_Renovation/)!
The [original version](https://store.steampowered.com/app/356670/Spookys_Jump_Scare_Mansion/) of the game can be played with a bit of knowledge and workarounds. Do note you'll need to own the [Karamari Hospital DLC](https://store.steampowered.com/app/414150/SJSM__Karamari_Hospital/) and the [Dollhouse DLC](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1390840/Spookys_Jump_Scare_Mansion__The_Doll_House/) to play.

Your main goal is to beat the Dollhouse DLC and get either the good or the bad ending.
- Bad Ending: Simply free Spooky's spirit and leave the mansion.
- Good Ending: Seal the Hellgate, free Spooky's spirit and leave the mansion.

The apworld expect the player to have beaten the Karamari Hospital DLC once to unlock New game+, reaching endless room 1000 (HD version) is optional.
[Crowd Control](https://crowdcontrol.live/) is optional as well, but heavily recommended to enable for chaos!

# 📝 Progression Items
- Progressive Elevator (11): Required to use the elevator per floor.
- Elevator Malfunction: Required to access the Karamari Hospital.
- Doll: Required to access the Dollhouse (Still need Elevator Malfunction).
- Progressive Endless Elevator (24): Required to explore the endless mansion (Reaches to 2500).
- Weapons (Axe, Sword, Gun)
- Arcade Machines
- Secret Code
